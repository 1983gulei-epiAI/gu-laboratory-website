People

A team built around experimental–computational integration

Our group brings together epigenomics, molecular and disease biology, bioinformatics and artificial intelligence. Training is organised around scientific ownership: each researcher is expected to connect a biological question to the evidence needed to test it.

Principal investigator

Lei Gu, PhD

Independent Group Leader at the Max Planck Institute for Heart and Lung Research since 2021.

The laboratory connects experimental epigenomics, causal disease biology and AI-driven computational biology. Lei Gu trained in bioinformatics and cancer epigenomics at DKFZ / Heidelberg University and subsequently worked at Boston Children’s Hospital and Harvard Medical School before establishing the group in Bad Nauheim.
