Research programs

Mechanisms, measurements and models of epigenetic regulation

Our research is organized around biological questions rather than techniques. Each program combines quantitative profiling with functional validation and computational modeling.

01

Epigenetics of regeneration

Why can some tissues regenerate efficiently while others lose this capacity? We investigate how chromatin accessibility, DNA-associated proteins and transcriptional regulatory networks determine cellular plasticity and regenerative competence, with a strong focus on the heart.

Core modalities
Single-cell / single-nucleus genomics · chromatin profiling · perturbation · functional cardiac models
02

Disease epigenomics

We study how disrupted chromatin states and regulatory circuits contribute to disease progression. Current and prior work spans cancer epigenomics, vascular inflammation and chronic disease, integrating patient-derived data with experimental models.

Questions
Cell-state transitions · therapy response · inflammation · regulatory vulnerabilities
03

Genome AI and computational biology

We develop machine-learning approaches for genomic sequence and epigenomic data, including long-context models and multimodal strategies. The goal is to capture regulatory grammar and make experimentally testable predictions.

Methods
Foundation models · representation learning · multimodal integration · interpretable prediction
04

Epigenetic inheritance and environmental memory

We investigate how environmental exposure can create persistent molecular states and how such information may cross developmental or generational boundaries. Experimental systems are selected to distinguish correlation, intergenerational exposure and true transgenerational inheritance.

Systems
Longitudinal cohorts · Drosophila · functional perturbation · multi-omics
05

Epigenomic technologies and computational tools

Method development is a recurring theme of the laboratory—from low-input whole-genome bisulfite sequencing to genomic visualization and quantitative analytical frameworks. We focus on enabling measurements that were previously impractical or difficult to interpret.

Track record
Low-input WGBS · genomic visualization · integrative omics · analytical benchmarking
06

Epigenetic biomarkers of biological aging

We use histone modifications and regulatory chromatin features to model biological age across tissues and species, then connect predictive signals back to mechanisms of senescence and tissue function.

Recent direction
Histone modification clocks · cross-species prediction · senescence regulation
