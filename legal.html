Data processing on this static website

The current site does not intentionally deploy advertising, behavioural profiling, analytics scripts, contact forms or first-party tracking cookies. Technical infrastructure used to deliver and secure the website may process request data such as IP address, date and time, requested resource, referrer and browser information. The exact controller/processor wording, legal basis, retention periods and international-transfer information must be finalised after the institutional provider status and hosting arrangement are confirmed.

External services

Links to services such as Google Maps, ORCID, Google Scholar, publication platforms and other external sites are standard outbound links. Data are transferred to those providers only when a visitor follows the relevant link; the external provider's privacy terms then apply.

Copyright and external links

Original text, graphics and other materials created for this website are protected by applicable copyright law unless otherwise stated. Third-party content remains subject to the rights and attribution requirements of its respective owner. External websites are the responsibility of their respective providers.

Pre-launch action

Before switching gulaboratory.com to this site, confirm with the institute administration or responsible communications/data-protection contact: (1) the legally responsible service provider, (2) the person responsible for editorial content, (3) whether the MPG institutional imprint and privacy notice can be adopted or linked, and (4) the required wording for Cloudflare-hosted request logs.
