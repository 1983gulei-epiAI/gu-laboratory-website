2026 · PUBLICATION

Histone modification clocks published in PNAS

Zhixin Niu, Chang Liu, Yurong Fan and Lei Gu report cross-tissue and cross-species histone modification clocks for biological age prediction and analysis of senescence regulation.

RECENT · FOUNDATION MODELS

JanusDNA establishes a bidirectional genomic foundation-model framework

Work led with collaborators introduces a hybrid bidirectional DNA foundation model for transferable genomic sequence representations and downstream prediction.

JULY 2026 · PREPRINT

M6AFormer released on bioRxiv

The preprint uses sequence-based modelling to prioritise previously unannotated functional m6A candidate sites for experimental follow-up.

2025–2028 · FUNDING

International collaboration targets COPD progression and lung cancer transition

The laboratory participates as Co-Investigator in an NSFC International Collaboration Fund for Creative Research Teams project combining AI and molecular diagnostics.
