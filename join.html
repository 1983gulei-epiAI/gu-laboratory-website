Join us

We look for people who can connect disciplines

We welcome applications from researchers interested in epigenetics, genomics, computational biology, AI, cardiovascular biology and disease mechanisms.

PHD

Doctoral researchers

Strong candidates typically bring depth in either experimental biology or computation and a clear motivation to develop the complementary skill set.

POSTDOC

Postdoctoral researchers

We particularly value candidates with a defined scientific question, evidence of independence and the ability to bridge methods with mechanism.

VISIT

Interns & visiting scientists

Focused projects are possible when there is a strong methodological or biological fit and a realistic project scope.

How to contact us

Please send a concise email describing: (1) your current training, (2) the scientific question you want to pursue, (3) why the Gu Lab is the right environment, and (4) your intended timing and funding status.

Attach a CV and, where relevant, one or two representative papers or preprints.

Fit matters more than a generic application.

A short, specific research rationale is substantially more informative than a long template cover letter.
