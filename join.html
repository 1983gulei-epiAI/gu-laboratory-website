PhD

Good projects usually start from a biological question and combine depth in either experimental or computational work with a willingness to learn across the interface.

Postdoc

We value applicants who can define a tractable scientific question, distinguish association from causality and build an independent line of work within the laboratory programme.

Visitors

Shorter projects are considered when the question, available data or experimental system and expected deliverable are sufficiently well defined.

What to send

A concise enquiry should state your current training, the scientific question you want to pursue, which part of the laboratory programme it connects to, your intended timing and—where relevant—your funding status.

Please attach a CV. For postdoctoral enquiries, include one or two representative papers or preprints and briefly state your own contribution.
