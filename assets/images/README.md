# Website image assets

Use this folder for laboratory-owned images used on gulaboratory.com.

## Folder structure
- `lab/` — group photos, retreats and laboratory scenes
- `people/` — PI and member portraits
- `research/` — laboratory-owned scientific figures and schematics

## Naming
Use lowercase English names with hyphens, for example:
- `group-2026.webp`
- `lei-gu.webp`
- `chang-liu.webp`
- `imotif-model.webp`

Avoid spaces, Chinese characters and camera-generated names such as `IMG_5839.JPG`.

## Recommended web sizes
- Member portraits: square, approximately 800 × 800 px
- PI portrait: square or 4:5, approximately 1200 px on the long edge
- Group photos: 1800–2400 px wide
- Research figures: 1200–1800 px wide
- Prefer WebP when practical; JPEG/PNG are acceptable when needed
- Keep ordinary photographs roughly 100–500 KB where image quality permits

## Copyright
Upload only images the laboratory has the right to publish. Do not migrate third-party stock imagery from the former website unless its licence explicitly permits reuse outside that platform.
