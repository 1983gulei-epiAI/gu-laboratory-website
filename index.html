Operating principle

One experimental–computational cycle, not separate wet- and dry-lab pipelines.

We develop measurements for regulatory states, use multimodal data to identify candidate mechanisms, test causality through perturbation and functional models, then train predictive models that are returned to the bench or evaluated in clinically relevant settings.

See the programme

Measure → model → perturb → validate → transfer

The same logic connects structural epigenomics, disease mechanisms, ageing trajectories and foundation models. Cardiovascular and pulmonary disease provide increasingly important physiological settings for this cycle.
