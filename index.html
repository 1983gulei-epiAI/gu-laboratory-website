Approach

We treat epigenetic regulation as a dynamic system that can be measured, modeled and experimentally perturbed.

Projects typically connect quantitative genomics with mechanistic experiments. This allows us to move from association to causal regulatory models—and from individual datasets to generalizable biological principles.
