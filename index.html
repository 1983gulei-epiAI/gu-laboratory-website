Approach

Measure → mechanism → causality → trajectory → prediction

We develop measurements for regulatory states, identify candidate mechanisms with multi-omic and spatial data, test causality through perturbation and disease models, and carry validated biological variables into longitudinal and predictive models.

Research

One experimental–computational cycle

Technology, mechanism and prediction are developed in one experimental–computational loop. Cancer, cardiovascular and pulmonary disease provide complementary settings in which regulatory states can be measured, perturbed and translated.
